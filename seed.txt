NVIDIA Corporation — Annual Report Summary (Seed Document)

Item 1. Business Overview
NVIDIA designs and sells accelerated computing platforms for AI, data centers, gaming, and automotive markets. The company's GPU architecture powers large-scale AI training and inference workloads worldwide. Key product lines include the A100, H100, and H200 data center GPUs, GeForce gaming GPUs, and the DRIVE platform for autonomous vehicles.

Item 1A. Risk Factors
Key risks include: concentration of revenue in a small number of customers, geopolitical export restrictions on advanced semiconductors, supply chain dependencies on third-party fabrication (primarily TSMC), rapid technology transitions requiring sustained R&D investment, and increasing competition from custom silicon (e.g., Google TPUs, Amazon Trainium).

Item 7. Financial Highlights
For fiscal year 2024, NVIDIA reported revenue of $60.9 billion, representing a 126% year-over-year increase driven primarily by Data Center segment growth. Data Center revenue reached $47.5 billion, up 217% from the prior year. Gross margin expanded to 72.7%. The company invested $8.7 billion in research and development.

Item 8. Segment Information
- Data Center: $47.5B revenue (78% of total) — AI training and inference platforms
- Gaming: $10.4B revenue (17% of total) — GeForce GPUs and console processors
- Professional Visualization: $1.6B revenue — Quadro/RTX workstation GPUs
- Automotive: $1.1B revenue — DRIVE platform and infotainment systems
